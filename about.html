About Koa

Who is Koa?

Makoa Kuuaki or more commonly known as Koa is a 3 year old shiba inu. He was born on February 15th 2022, and he was adopted in May 2023. Koa is a widly energetic and playful shiba inu with a slim figure. He is a dog of many names, which he selectively responds to only.

The Physical Characteristics of Koa

To the untrained eye, koa could be confused with an other shiba inu. Although a true Koa lover can easily distinguish him in a crowd of other shibas. Some defining characteristics that you can use to pin point koa:

  • Koa has a large beauty mark on the right side of his face
  • Koa has large brown eyes, and you can only occassionaly see the whites of his eyes
  • Koa's has two small black eye brows
  • Koa has a tuft in the center of his chest
  • Koa is 35lbs
  • Koa has a curly tail that sometimes rests half curled on his back
