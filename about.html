About Koa

Who is Koa?

Makoa Kuuaki or more commonly known as Koa is a 3 year old shiba inu. He was born on February 15th 2022, and he was adopted in May 2023. Koa is a widly energetic and playful shiba inu with a slim figure. He is a dog of many names, which he selectively responds to only.

The Physical Characteristics of Koa

To the untrained eye, koa could be confused with any other shiba inu. Although a true Koa lover can easily distinguish him in a crowd of other shibas. Some defining characteristics that you can use to identify koa:

  • large beauty mark on the right side of his face
  • large brown eyes (aka boba eyes)
  • two small black eye brows
  • small tuft in the center of his chest
  • 35lbs and a slim build
  • curly tail that sometimes rests half curled on his back

The Immense Personality Koa

Koa's unique personality is what makes him different than any other dog. He is insanely spirited, loving, and funny. As Koa has grown up his personality has blossomed with him. Some unique personality attributes that make Koa special consist of:

  • Inquisitive, Koa is a very curious dog always looking to see what your doing or what you may have in your hands
  • Compassionate, Koa is a very compassionate dog who will always notice when your sad or stressed and find ways to cheer you up!
  • Fearful, as inquisitive as Koa is he is very fearful often being scared of the tiny noises or movements such as a box falling or the doorbell ringing
  • Persistant, Koa is incredibly persistant often times making him struggle to take no for an answer. Once he sets his mind to something it is very difficult to stop him.
